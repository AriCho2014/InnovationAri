About Engineering Club

New Vistas Center for Education Engineering Club: Innovation

What New Vistas Engineering Club Does

Their club members brainstorm, design, and build projects that connect classroom concepts to hands-on experiences. Students explore topics like robotics, architecture, environmental design, and technology-driven solutions.

Their Goals
  • Encourage creative problem solving and critical thinking.
  • Develop engineering skills through collaboration and experimentation.
  • Foster teamwork and leadership among students of 4-6 grades.
  • Present their findings and creations to the school community.
Want to Join?

Engineering Club meets after school twice every week to plan projects and conduct experiments.
