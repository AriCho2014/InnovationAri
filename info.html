About the Innovation Club

New Vistas Center for Education Engineering Club: Innovation

The Innovation Club at New Vistas Center for Education encourages students to apply creativity and engineering thinking to solve real-world problems.

What We Do

Our club members brainstorm, design, and build projects that connect classroom concepts to hands-on experiences. Students explore topics like robotics, architecture, environmental design, and technology-driven solutions.

Our Goals

• Encourage creative problem solving and critical thinking.
• Develop engineering skills through collaboration and experimentation.
• Foster teamwork and leadership among students of all grades.
• Present our findings and creations to the school community.

Want to Join?

Engineering Club meets after school twice every week to plan projects and conduct experiments.
